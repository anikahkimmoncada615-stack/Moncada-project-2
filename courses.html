College of Education, Arts and Sciences

The College of Education, Arts and Sciences focuses on providing students with quality education, knowledge, and skills that help them become competent professionals and responsible members of society. It develops students' critical thinking, communication, creativity, leadership, and problem-solving abilities through classroom learning, practical activities, and academic experiences.

The college prepares future educators and professionals who can contribute to the development of their communities. It also promotes lifelong learning, good moral values, social responsibility, and a deeper understanding of different fields of knowledge.

COLLEGE OF
MANAGEMENT,
ACCOUNTANCY
AND TECHNOLOGY
(CMAT)

College of Management, Accountancy and Technology

The College of Management, Accountancy and Technology provides students with knowledge and skills in business, management, accounting, and technology. It helps students understand how organizations operate, how financial resources are managed, and how technology can be used to improve productivity and efficiency.

Students develop leadership, analytical thinking, decision-making, communication, and entrepreneurial skills through academic activities and practical learning. The college prepares graduates for careers in business, office administration, accounting, management, and technology-related fields, while encouraging innovation, professionalism, and responsible work practices.

COLLEGE OF
CRIMINOLOGY

CRIMINOLOGY

College of Criminology

The College of Criminology focuses on the study of crime, criminal behavior, law enforcement, criminal justice, and public safety. It provides students with knowledge about the causes of crime, the legal system, crime prevention, investigation, and the importance of maintaining peace and order in society.

Students develop discipline, critical thinking, observation, communication, leadership, and investigative skills. The program prepares students for careers in law enforcement, public safety, corrections, and other criminal justice-related professions. It also emphasizes integrity, respect for human rights, accountability, and service to the community.
